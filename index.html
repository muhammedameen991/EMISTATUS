THE STORY

Are we buying a phone — or a status symbol?

A premium smartphone can be a useful product, but the way it is marketed can make a large purchase feel much smaller when the headline focuses on a monthly EMI.

EMI itself is not automatically good or bad. What matters is the complete picture: income, essential expenses, existing debt, savings, interest, fees and other financial goals.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is buying an iPhone on EMI always a bad idea?

No. Affordability depends on income, essential expenses, savings, existing debt and the actual financing terms.

Does EMI STATUS provide financial advice?

No. EMI STATUS provides general educational information and calculator estimates. It does not provide individualized financial advice.

Why can the calculator differ from my lender?

Actual repayment can vary because of interest methods, fees, taxes, discounts, tenure and lender-specific terms.
